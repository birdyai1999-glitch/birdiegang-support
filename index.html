BirdieGang Support

Privacy Policy and Support

Last updated: May 27, 2026

BirdieGang is a golf scoring app for creating rooms, inviting players, tracking rounds, and reviewing completed golf games.

Information We Collect

BirdieGang collects account information such as display name, email address, Supabase user ID, and an app-generated device identifier. BirdieGang also collects gameplay information such as room codes, player rosters, ready state, scores, strokes, penalties, round state, and round history.

If you choose a profile photo, it is used for your profile experience. The app may use the camera to scan a room QR code.

How We Use Information

We use this information to provide login, password reset, multiplayer rooms, live scoring, course setup, score history, and account support.

BirdieGang does not sell personal information. BirdieGang does not track users across other companies' apps or websites for advertising.

Service Providers

BirdieGang uses Supabase for authentication, database, and realtime room services. The app may call golf course data services to show course information.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep account and gameplay information for as long as needed to provide the app. To request account deletion or data deletion, contact support at birdyai1999@gmail.com.

Support

For privacy questions, account deletion, password reset help, or general support, email birdyai1999@gmail.com.
